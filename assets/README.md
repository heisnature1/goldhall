# Gold Refinery Hall media assets

Add the hall's own images to the folders below. Keep the suggested filenames so each image can be connected to the correct part of the website easily.

## Folder and filename guide

| Website area | Save image here | Suggested filename | Recommended size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hall logo | `assets/logo/` | `gold-refinery-hall-logo.png` | Square PNG, at least 512 × 512 px; transparent background preferred |
| Home hero slide 1 | `assets/images/hero/` | `hero-hall-building.jpg` | 1920 × 1080 px or larger, landscape |
| Home hero slide 2 | `assets/images/hero/` | `hero-campus-life.jpg` | 1920 × 1080 px or larger, landscape |
| Home hero slide 3 | `assets/images/hero/` | `hero-students.jpg` | 1920 × 1080 px or larger, landscape |
| About section | `assets/images/about/` | `hall-building.jpg` | 1200 × 900 px, landscape |
| Hall Master portrait | `assets/images/leadership/` | `hall-master.jpg` | 800 × 800 px, square portrait |
| Senior Tutor portrait | `assets/images/leadership/` | `senior-tutor.jpg` | 800 × 800 px, square portrait |
| JCR President portrait | `assets/images/leadership/` | `jcr-president.jpg` | 800 × 800 px, square portrait |
| News story images | `assets/images/news/` | `news-01.jpg`, `news-02.jpg`, etc. | 1200 × 800 px, landscape |
| Event images | `assets/images/events/` | `event-01.jpg`, `event-02.jpg`, etc. | 1200 × 800 px, landscape |
| Gallery images | `assets/images/gallery/` | `gallery-01.jpg`, `gallery-02.jpg`, etc. | 1600 × 1200 px or larger |

## Image rules

- Use `.jpg` for photographs. Use `.png` for the logo or images requiring transparency.
- Use lowercase filenames with hyphens only—no spaces or special characters.
- Compress photos before adding them. Aim for less than 500 KB per standard image and less than 1 MB per hero image.
- Do not overwrite a published image without keeping a backup of the original.

When the images have been added, use paths such as `assets/images/gallery/gallery-01.jpg` in the Admin Portal's **Image URL** field. The website can then display the local image.
