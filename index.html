About Us

Our Heritage & Legacy

Est. 2005

A legacy of excellence

Building Leaders Since 2005

Gold Refinery Hall stands as one of the prestigious halls of residence at the University of Mines and Technology, Tarkwa. Named after the rich gold mining heritage of the Western Region of Ghana, the hall embodies the spirit of transformation and excellence.

Our mission is to provide a conducive living and learning environment that nurtures academic excellence, personal development, and community engagement among students.
